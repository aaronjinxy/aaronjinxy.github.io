FAQ

Which Garmin watches are supported?

Any modern Garmin watch that supports Connect IQ watch faces (Fenix / Epix / Forerunner / Venu / Instinct / Enduro series and others). If the face installs, it works.

Where do I find the 6-digit device code?

Switch to the watch face on your watch. Before activation, a red watermark with a 6-digit number is displayed on the screen. Enter that number at checkout.

How fast will I receive my activation code?

Automatically, usually within one minute of payment, sent to the email address on your order. Check your spam folder if you don't see it.

What if the code doesn't work?

Just reply to the delivery email with the 6-digit number from the red watermark — a corrected code is sent automatically. We stand behind every purchase.
