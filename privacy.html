Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 2026

1. Who we are

YuanCao Watch Faces ("we", "us") sells digital watch face activation codes. Payments on our checkout pages are processed by Paddle.com Market Ltd, acting as Merchant of Record. This policy explains what personal data we handle and why.

2. What we collect

  • Payment details – collected and stored by Paddle only. We never see or store your card number.

3. What we do NOT collect

We do not use advertising or analytics trackers on this site, and we do not sell your data to anyone.

4. Third parties

Order and email records are kept for up to 3 years for accounting and support purposes, then deleted.
