Refund Policy

Effective date: November 2026

1. Our promise

If your activation code does not work on your watch, we will first send you a corrected code. If it still does not work, you get a full refund – no questions asked.

2. When you can request a refund

  • The activation code does not work and we cannot provide a working one within 7 days of your report.
  • You purchased by mistake or duplicated an order – tell us within 14 days of purchase and the code has not been activated.
  • EU/EEA/UK consumers: you additionally have the statutory 14-day right of withdrawal for digital content, which you waive when the code is delivered and activated. If you have not activated the code, the statutory right applies in full.

3. How to request

Email yuancao000@163.com with your transaction ID (found in your receipt from Paddle). Refunds are issued via Paddle to your original payment method, usually within 3–5 business days.

4. What is not refundable

  • Codes already activated and working on your watch.
  • Requests made more than 14 days after purchase where the code works as described.
